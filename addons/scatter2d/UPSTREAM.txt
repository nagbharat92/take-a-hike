Edge (Even) modifier and original Domain/TransformList/Polygon resources.
One persistent disabled-output Scatter2D node supplies its domain transform;
the project's existing grounded sprite renderer consumes the returned transforms.
No ScatterItem instancing, addon thread, particles or editor plugin is enabled.

Poisson evaluated but not used: upstream documents disconnected-domain and
retry/cost limits. Its flat-grid indexing also uses grid_size.y as the stride.
The hike uses narrow projected ground ribbons, so independent per-grove
Curve2D edge sampling fits this geometry without a Poisson compatibility fork.
Built-in FastNoiseLite varies the broad grove-domain widths; exact root support,
rest exclusions, boot/stair clearance and canopy clearance remain authoritative.
